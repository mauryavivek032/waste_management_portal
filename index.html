DISPOSE RESPONSIBLY

E-waste & recycling guide

Choose the right next step for common waste types.

🍌
ORGANIC

Food & garden waste

Keep food scraps separate from plastic and other dry waste. Use a composting or authorised organic-waste service if available.

Best route Compost / local biogas
🧴
PLASTIC

Bottles & packaging

Empty and rinse containers when appropriate. Follow local recycling rules because accepted plastic types vary by facility.

Best route Accepted recycling stream
📄
PAPER

Paper & cardboard

Keep dry and separate from food residue. Flatten boxes to reduce storage and transport volume.

Best route Paper recycling
🥫
METAL

Cans & scrap metal

Separate clean metal items and use an appropriate scrap dealer or recycling collection point.

Best route Metal recycler
🔋
SPECIAL HANDLING

Batteries & electronics

Do not place batteries or electronics in ordinary mixed waste. Avoid dismantling batteries; use an authorised e-waste collection point or take-back programme.

Best route Authorised e-waste handler
🧺
MIXED

Uncertain materials

If you're unsure what an item is made from, keep it separate until you can check local disposal guidance.

Best route Check local guidance
